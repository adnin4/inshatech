Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our multi-agent architecture and data privacy.

Why self-hosted n8n over Zapier or Make?

Self-hosted n8n runs entirely within your private cloud. This means zero per-task software fees as your transaction volume scales, 100% HIPAA/GDPR data privacy compliance, and zero vendor lock-in.

How does the 4-Level Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) system work?

Safe operations (Level 0 and 1) execute and draft automatically. Any critical, public, or destructive actions (Level 2 and 3: sending emails, updating CRM status, changing pricing) require explicit human approval via our dashboard.

How fast is the turnaround time?

Single workflows and starter builds are live in production within 5 to 7 business days. Complete interconnected multi-agent suites deploy in 2 to 3 weeks.

Do I own the workflows, code, and prompts?

Yes, 100%. All n8n workflows, database schemas, prompt pipelines, and integrations belong entirely to your company. You retain complete IP ownership.
