## 🌊 2. The 12 Locked Execution Phases

1. **Phase 1 — Foundation**: Multi-tenant database schemas with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), RBAC, session auth, and cryptographic token brokers.
2. **Phase 2 — AI Control Plane**: Centralized C-Panel management for Agent Registry, Model Router, Tool Manifests, and 5-Level Permissions (L0–L4).
3. **Phase 3 — Execution Plane**: Mission Engine, durable multi-step workflows, agent orchestration, and ephemeral sandboxed execution.
4. **Phase 4 — Business OS**: CRM pipeline, dynamic service studio, order state machines, and invoice generation.
5. **Phase 5 — Affiliate & Partner OS**: Server-side attribution, click tracking, 28-parameter partner lifecycle, fraud detection, and automated payouts.
6. **Phase 6 — Intelligence & Digital Twin**: Universal Copilot 4.0, AI Revenue Analyst, AI Coach, and Digital Twin 2.0 Scenario Lab.
7. **Phase 7 — Safety & Governance**: Guardian Agent, fraud detection, Owner Approval Inbox, tamper-resistant audit logs, and the Global Emergency Kill-Switch.
8. **Phase 8 — Observability & Telemetry**: OpenTelemetry tracing, live agent activity stream, replayable mission timeline, and Continuous AI Evaluation Lab.
9. **Phase 9 — Automation & Incidents**: 3-tier notification bus (`🟢 Info`, `🟡 Important`, `🔴 Critical`), automated rate-limit buffering, and self-healing circuit breakers.
10. **Phase 10 — Scale & Multi-Tenancy**: Feature flags (`0% ➔ 10% ➔ 50% ➔ 100%`), tenant-aware logging, OpenAPI 3.1 contracts, and webhook routers.
11. **Phase 11 — Production Hardening**: Automated synthetic red-teaming, disaster recovery drills (RPO < 15 min, RTO < 5 min), and canary rollback guards.
12. **Phase 12 — Continuous Optimization**: Conversion rate optimization, partner tier upgrades, token context budgets, and quantitative AI labour ROI tracking.
