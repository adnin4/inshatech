## 7-Day Step-by-Step Action Plan

### Day 1: Asset Audit & 50-Lead ICP List Building
* **Goal:** Build a targeted list of 50 high-intent prospects across 3 distinct verticals.
* **Target Breakdown:**
  1. **20 Digital Marketing / Web Agencies:** Look for agencies offering web design or SEO but lacking in-house AI engineering. Pitch: *Silent White-Label Technical Delivery Pod*.
  2. **15 Early-Stage B2B SaaS Founders:** Check Product Hunt, IndieHackers, and Twitter/X for founders with active backlogs. Pitch: *Rapid 24-48hr SaaS Bug Fixing & AI Feature Pilots*.
  3. **15 E-Commerce / Local Service Businesses:** Real estate brokerages, medical clinics, or Shopify stores. Pitch: *60-Second Lead Response & WhatsApp Booking Agent*.
* **Tools to Use:**
  - LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Google Maps / Twitter Advanced Search.
  - Record company name, decision-maker name (Founder, CEO, Head of Growth), verified email/LinkedIn profile, and their specific friction point.

---

### Day 2: The 2-Minute Video Teardown Sprint
* **Goal:** Record 10 hyper-personalized 90-to-120 second Loom videos demonstrating their exact bottleneck and your live working solution.
* **The 4-Step Loom Video Script:**
  1. **The Hook (0–15s):** Show their website or software. *"Hey [Name], I noticed you have an inbound lead form on your site, but when a customer submits after hours, there's a 4-hour delay before contact."*
  2. **The Problem (15–40s):** *"In your industry, conversion rates drop by 391% if leads aren't answered within 60 seconds."*
  3. **The Proof (40–80s):** Switch tabs to your live IINSHA showcase. Show the `<insha-3d-visual>` workflow or demo the WhatsApp Concierge / Gemini OCR line parser in real time. *"We built an automated agent that ingests the submission, verifies the phone number, and fires an instant personalized WhatsApp message in 45 seconds."*
  4. **The No-Risk Call to Action (80–110s):** *"I'm not asking you to buy a \$5,000 system. We offer a 48-hour pilot for \$350 where we plug this into your test environment. If it doesn't shave hours off your workflow, you pay nothing. Would it make sense to chat for 10 minutes Thursday?"*

### Day 4: Discovery Calls & Interactive Architecture Demos
* **Goal:** Convert 3–5 inbound replies into 15-minute discovery calls.
* **Call Structure (15 Minutes):**
  - **Min 0–3:** Rapport & Understanding their primary daily bottleneck.
  - **Min 3–8:** Live Demo on `inshatech-b6b.pages.dev`. Walk them through the interactive architecture canvas, the 14-role RBAC matrix, and live code examples.
  - **Min 8–12:** Scope definition: Define 1 specific, high-impact deliverable (e.g., "Extract 500 competitor prices daily" or "Automate invoice PDF ingestion into Google Sheets").
  - **Min 12–15:** Present the Pilot Price (\$350–\$850) and timeline (48–72 hours). Send proposal link immediately.

---

### Day 5: The 1-Page Milestone-Gated Proposal
* **Goal:** Send clean, 1-page proposals within 2 hours of each call.
* **Proposal Template:**
  1. **Objective:** Automate [Specific Process] to save [X hours/week].
  2. **Deliverables:**
     - Modular script / workflow deployed to client VPS or Cloudflare Workers.
     - Playwright automated regression test confirming zero errors.
     - 1-hour Loom video documentation + code handover.
  3. **Timeline:** 48 hours from escrow confirmation.
  4. **Investment:** \$450 USD (or equivalent BDT).
  5. **Risk Reversal:** Milestone-Gated Acceptance Guarantee. 50% deposit into escrow, 50% upon successful client UAT.

---

### Day 6: Escrow Deposit & Sprint Kickoff
* **Goal:** Collect payment and kick off technical execution.
* **Execution Protocol:**

  - Create isolated Git branch: `feat/client-[clientname]-[feature]`.
  - Implement solution surgically.
  - Run regression test suite (`npm test`, Playwright).
  - Record 2-minute walkthrough showing the test passing on client data.

## Objection Handling Quick Reference

| Objection | Why They Say It | Your Response |
|:---|:---|:---|
| *"AI hallucinates and makes mistakes."* | Fear of public embarrassment or inaccurate client data. | *"Exactly why we don't rely on raw LLM prompts. We use multi-document RAG with vector grounding, strict source citation, and automated human-in-the-loop escalation gates when confidence falls below 95%."* |
| *"We already have an internal developer."* | Defensive posture regarding existing payroll. | *"That's great! We don't replace your devs—we act as a silent burst pod. When your team is focused on core product, we handle the repetitive scraping, webhook glue, and background scripts without adding payroll overhead."* |
| *"Your price is higher than a freelancer on Upwork."* | Testing your conviction and ROI justification. | *"Upwork freelancers often leave you with unmaintained code and zero test suites. We deliver production systems backed by 4-browser Playwright regression tests, full documentation, and a milestone acceptance guarantee."* |
| *"What if it doesn't work for our workflow?"* | Fear of sunk financial cost. | *"That's why we structure pilots with Milestone-Gated Acceptance. You review the staging demo on your actual data before final sign-off."* |
